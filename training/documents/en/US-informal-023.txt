u/two_flat_tom · 2d
The RLTO requires the itemized statement within 30 days of move-out with receipts or estimates. Did he include receipts? If not, that's already a problem. Also yes, interest is required. Look up [[ORG|Metropolitan Tenants Organization]], they have a hotline and they're great.
