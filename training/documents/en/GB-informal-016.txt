Re: by Hettie_M
Yes, send a letter before claim giving 14 days. Keep it to facts: dates, hours, amount. Send it recorded to the registered office, check Companies House because it might not be the takeaway address. When I did this with my old boss at [[ORG|Bulwell Kitchens]] the registered office turned out to be his accountant in [[LOC|Beeston]].
